# Backdrops

Pictures the DM shows the table **instead of** the board — a forested clearing, a campsite, the
inside of a tavern — for the stretches of an evening where there is nothing to move and nothing
to measure. Drop PNG, JPEG or WebP files in here, subfolders and all, and they appear in the
table panel under **show a backdrop…**.

A backdrop is not a map, and that is the whole of what makes this folder worth having rather
than a corner of `maps/`. There is no grid on it, nothing stands on it, nothing is traced across
it and nobody explores it. Loading a *map* sweeps the board — the walls, the drawings and
everywhere the party has been — because a new image is a new dungeon. Putting a picture in front
of the table sweeps nothing: the encounter is still there, untouched, and taking the backdrop
down puts everyone back exactly where they were. See *Backdrop* in `docs/maps.md`.

Sized like a map rather than like a portrait, since it fills the window. It is drawn to fit,
centred, whole — no cropping and no camera — so the shape of the image is up to you and letterbox
bars are normal.

Picking one **copies it into the uploads directory** rather than serving this folder, the same
rule maps and portraits follow. Unlike those two there is no upload button: the folder is the
collection, which is also what makes "a few presets" cost nothing to keep.

`dusk.png` is a placeholder from `tools/gen-assets.mjs`, here so the picker is not an empty list on
a fresh clone. Delete it once there is real art in the folder.

`maps/` already holds several images that work well here — the two *Forest Encampment* files in
particular. Copy them across rather than picking them as maps.

Point this somewhere else with `SLATE_BACKDROPS`.
